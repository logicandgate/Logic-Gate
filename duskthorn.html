The South Asylum

Many years ago, a large asylum stood deep in the forest, far from anyone who might ask questions. It was a working psychiatric hospital — doctors, nurses, patients, and a research wing whose work never appeared in any report.

Evelyn

Evelyn Marsh was a staff nurse on the ward. She fell in love with one of the asylum's doctors, Dr. A. Holloway, and she trusted him completely. Soon, he told her, they would let the others know about them. She believed him. She believed everything he said, and she thought they would spend their lives together.

The Holloway Treatment

Holloway's experiments needed results, and the board would not fund them without results by spring. Evelyn offered to help. She would do anything for him. She became his subject.

Her patient record survived. In the ninth session her heart stopped twice, and she was brought back. By the eleventh she was asking for him by his first name and calling him “my love.” In the twelfth, she did not wake. Her cause of death was written down as fever. Her body was never released to her family, and the truth about the experiments was buried with her.

What Stayed Behind

Something remained inside the asylum. The staff began to hear footsteps on empty wards and a woman humming in the corridors. They saw a nurse in uniform standing where no one should be. Holloway heard her too — humming the song he used to whistle on the ward, standing at the foot of his bed, saying his name. She was not angry, he wrote. That was the worst of it. She still loved him.

One by one the staff left. The asylum was abandoned and closed for good, and it became a place of rumours that nobody dared to enter.

Her Kiss

Her love for the doctor who killed her has twisted into an obsession. She greets every intruder the way she once greeted him — with a kiss. It is the last tender thing left in her, and the moment she takes hold of you, it turns deadly. The last page of Holloway's journal ends with a warning: if anyone finds this, do not let her kiss you.

Tonight

Years later, Ryan Cole of Cole After Dark goes live from the South Asylum's doors, with thousands of viewers daring him in. He has no idea what is waiting for him. The nurse is still there — and she has not forgotten the man she loved.
