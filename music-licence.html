3. How the Works were created

The Works were created on 14 September 2026 for the Owner's games Znite Run and Tanzanite Mining. They were not recorded, sampled, copied or adapted from any existing music, sound library, loop pack or recording. Every sound was synthesised mathematically by computer programs written for this purpose. The programs build each sound from basic waveforms (sine, sawtooth, square and noise) and arrange them according to an original composition. The creative choices — style, mood, instruments, structure, tempo, key, melody and arrangement — were directed by the Owner to match the story of Znite Run (Pluto, year 2200, AIOT City and the Broom Industries robot) and the style of Tanzanite Mining. The programs were written with the assistance of an AI coding tool used by the Owner. The terms of service of that tool provide that the user receives all rights, title and interest the tool provider may have in the output.
