Delete Your Data

Dreadhush: House of Silence — published by Logic & Gate

This page explains how to have data relating to your use of Dreadhush: House of Silence (package com.logicandgate.dreadhushproject) deleted, what gets deleted, and what is kept.

Dreadhush has no user accounts and no game server. Your progress never leaves your phone, and Logic & Gate does not hold a profile, an account or a save file for you. Most of what follows you can do yourself, immediately, without asking us.

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@logicandgate.com from any address.
  2. Use the subject line Data deletion request — Dreadhush.
  3. Tell us what you want deleted. If your request concerns advertising data, include your Android Advertising ID if you are able to (Android Settings → Google → Ads). We do not need it to act on emails you have sent us.
  4. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataHeld byWhat happens
Game progress, nights completed, settings, ad-consent choice Your device only Deleted the moment you uninstall the game or clear its data (Settings → Apps → Dreadhush → Storage → Clear data). We never receive it and cannot delete it for you.
Advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system, ad requests, impressions, taps, rewarded-ad completions, ad software diagnostics Unity Technologies (our advertising partner) Deleted on request to Unity. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads, which breaks the link to any past advertising data. You may also contact Unity directly through the Unity Player Privacy Policy. Unity keeps this data for the periods stated in its own policy. Email us and we will help you make the request.
Emails you have sent to our support address, and our replies Logic & Gate Deleted on request. If you do not ask, these are kept only as long as needed to help you, and in any case no longer than 12 months.

What we keep after a deletion request

Nothing, other than what the law requires us to retain. We may keep a minimal record that a deletion request was made and completed, so that we can show we honoured it. That record contains no advertising identifiers and no game data.

Your other rights

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, port or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. You can change your advertising choice at any time from the home screen of the game. Use the same address above for any of these requests.
