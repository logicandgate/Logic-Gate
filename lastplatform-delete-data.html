Delete Your Data

Last updated 21 November 2026

This page explains what The Last Platform (package com.logicandgate.thelastplatform) stores, how to delete it, and what stays with Google when you do. It applies to the release version, which uses Google AdMob for ads and Google Play for the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

The Game has no account and no game server. Your progress, journal, settings and ad-free entitlement live in the Game's private storage on your phone. We do not hold a profile or a save file for you, so most of this you can do yourself, immediately.

Delete your local data

Any one of these removes the save, the backup, your settings, the ad-free entitlement on that device, and your advertising consent choice:

  • Start a new game. The main menu's New Game replaces the current save.
  • Clear the Game's data. Android Settings › Apps › The Last Platform › Storage › Clear data.
  • Uninstall the Game. This removes the same local data from your phone.

Deleting local data does not cancel or refund the Remove Ads purchase. The purchase belongs to your Google Play account and can be restored when you reinstall.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted from your phone

  • the current shift, task progress, journal entries and evidence;
  • story choices and flags, such as stamped or refused tickets;
  • volume, music and text-sound settings;
  • the local record that Remove Ads is active;
  • your advertising consent choice.

Kept by Google

Advertising data. The free version uses Google AdMob. AdMob holds advertising data under its own policies; we never receive your advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and change ad personalization, in Android Settings › Google › Ads. You can also use the privacy controls on an ad or in the Game's Settings under Privacy options.

The Remove Ads purchase. Google Play holds the purchase record and payment details. We never see your card. Deleting the Game does not delete the purchase; it remains available to the Google account that bought it. Refunds and payment questions are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

Kept by us

Support emails you have sent us, and our replies, are kept for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them. We do not hold any other personal data about you.

Ask us to delete support emails

  1. Email support@logicandgate.com.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Last Platform data”.
  3. Tell us what you want removed. We will confirm within 30 days.

We cannot delete a save file or an ad ID for you because we never receive them. Use the device and Google controls above for those.
