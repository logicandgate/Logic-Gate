The Last Platform Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Solayman Islam Ruman, doing business as Logic & Gate (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you play The Last Platform, our mystery game for Android (the “Game”). Questions: support@logicandgate.com.

In short: The Last Platform has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Your progress and settings are saved only in the Game's own storage on your phone, and we never receive them. This policy describes the current development build; if anything changes before release, this page will be updated first.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Logic & Gate, a sole proprietorship owned by Solayman Islam Ruman, South Bhitabaria, Bhandaria, Pirojpur, Barisal Division 8550, Bangladesh. We are responsible for the information described in this policy. The Game is not connected to a server, so in practice the only information that exists is on your device.

2. What is stored on your phone

The Game saves your progress in its own private storage on your phone:

  • the current shift and how far through its task list you are;
  • the journal entries and evidence you have discovered;
  • story choices and flags, such as whether you stamped or refused a ticket and whether a passenger has left the platform;
  • your settings: master volume, music volume, sound-effects volume and the text-blip toggle.

This data is written to a save file with a backup copy in the Game's app-private folder. It is never sent to us, and no other app can read it on a normal Android device. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

3. What we do not collect

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account details. There is no registration, no login, no chat and no in-app purchase. The Game does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, device identifiers or advertising ID, and it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed, and in any case no longer than 12 months.

4. Internet and permissions

The Game does not request the Android internet permission and does not need a connection to play, save or load. It requests no runtime permissions at all. The whole game is playable offline.

5. Advertising and purchases

The current build contains no advertising, no ad SDK and no purchases. The plan is for the released game to be free to download. If advertising or an optional remove-ads purchase is added before release, this policy will be updated to name every SDK, the data it collects, its purpose and its retention period before that build is published, and the Game will remain playable if an ad fails to load or the device is offline.

6. Who information is shared with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because the Game does not send us any. The only exception is information you choose to send us yourself by email, which is handled as described in section 3.

7. Children

The Last Platform is a mystery game with mild unease. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

You are in control of the save because it never leaves your phone:

  • Start over in the Game. The main menu offers a new game, which replaces the current save.
  • Clear the Game's data. Android Settings › Apps › The Last Platform › Storage › Clear data. This deletes the save, the backup and the settings.
  • Uninstall the Game. This removes all of the above from your phone.
  • Emails you sent us. Ask us to delete them at support@logicandgate.com and we will do so within 30 days.

We cannot delete a save file for you because we never receive it.

9. Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if advertising, analytics or an account is added — we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released. The current policy describes a build with no data collection and no internet access.
