The Last Platform Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Solayman Islam Ruman, doing business as Logic & Gate (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you play The Last Platform, our mystery game for Android (the “Game”). The Terms and Conditions explain the licence, ads and purchases. Questions: support@logicandgate.com.

In short: The Game has no account of its own. Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The free version is supported by ads served through Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising information to deliver and measure those ads, and the free version needs an internet connection. A one-time Remove Ads purchase through Google Play removes the ads and lets you play offline. We never receive your payment card details.

This policy describes the current ad-supported build. The Remove Ads product is set up in Google Play; until it is, the purchase may be unavailable.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Logic & Gate, a sole proprietorship owned by Solayman Islam Ruman, South Bhitabaria, Bhandaria, Pirojpur, Barisal Division 8550, Bangladesh. We are responsible for the information described in this policy that we control. Our advertising and payment partners are responsible for the information they collect under their own privacy policies (see sections 3 and 8).

2. What is stored on your phone

The Game saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: the current shift and how far through its task list you are;
  • the journal entries and evidence you have discovered;
  • story choices and flags, such as whether you stamped or refused a ticket and whether a passenger has left the platform;
  • your settings: master volume, music volume, sound-effects volume and the text-blip toggle;
  • whether the Remove Ads purchase is active on this device;
  • your advertising consent choice, so the Game does not ask again on every launch.

Progress and settings are written to a save file with a backup copy in the Game's app-private folder. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes them.

3. What Google AdMob collects

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and its affiliates. To request, deliver, measure and protect those ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process:

  • your Android advertising ID (a resettable identifier), and your IP address;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address (not GPS);
  • device information such as model, operating system, language, screen size and network type;
  • app information such as the Game's package name and version;
  • ad requests, impressions, clicks, and interactions with the ad close or privacy controls;
  • diagnostic and fraud-prevention data about the ad request and the device.

Google uses this information to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations. If you consent to personalized ads, Google may use this data to choose ads based on your interests and activity across sites and apps. If you do not consent, the Game requests non-personalized ads where required.

We do not receive your advertising ID or your individual ad data from Google. We may see aggregate reports, such as how many ads were shown and estimated revenue. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

4. Ads, consent and your choices

Where the law requires it — including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the Game asks for your consent before using personalized advertising, through Google's consent management tools. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the Game's Privacy page, under AD PRIVACY.

You can also control advertising at the device level:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads;
  • turn off ad personalization in the same Android settings;
  • use the privacy icon on an ad to learn about that ad and its provider.

If you are in a US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to opt out of targeted advertising or of the “sharing” of personal information. Use the consent choices in the Game or email us at support@logicandgate.com. We do not sell personal information for money.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent — for personalized advertising and for storing your advertising choice on the device, where the law requires it.
  • Legitimate interests — for non-personalized advertising, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud and keeping the Game secure.
  • Contract — for processing the Remove Ads purchase and restoring it to your account.
  • Legal obligation — for records we are required to keep and for answering lawful requests.

5. Internet connection

The free version of the Game requires a working internet connection so that ads can be requested and shown. Without a connection, the free version may not be playable. The Game does not upload your save, journal or choices; the connection is used for advertising and for Google Play purchase checks.

The Remove Ads purchase removes the advertising requirement. Once the purchase has been confirmed and the entitlement is stored on your device, you can play without a connection.

6. The Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we do not receive or store your card number, billing address or other payment details. We receive the purchase status and the information needed to unlock the ad-free version, such as whether the purchase is active.

  • The purchase is tied to the Google account used in Google Play and can be restored after reinstalling the Game or on another device signed in to that account.
  • The Game stores the ad-free entitlement on your device so it can work offline after confirmation.
  • Refunds and payment disputes are handled under Google Play's refund policy. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.
  • No subscription is created and no recurring charge is made.

The Terms and Conditions set out the purchase rules, restoration and refunds.

7. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed, and in any case no longer than 12 months.

8. Who information is shared with

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the Game:

  • Google AdMob and its advertising partners, for the ad data described in section 3, under their own policies.
  • Google Play, for billing, purchase restoration and fraud prevention.
  • Authorities, where we are legally required to disclose information we hold. In practice we hold almost nothing: the save stays on your phone and we do not hold an account for you.

Where personalized advertising is enabled with your consent, some US state laws may treat that as “sharing” or “targeted advertising”. You can opt out as described in section 4.

9. International transfers

Google processes ad and purchase data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under its own safeguards. We do not operate a server and do not transfer your save data anywhere.

10. How long information is kept

Google keeps ad and purchase data for the periods described in its own policies. We keep aggregate ad reports only as long as needed to understand the Game's performance and meet accounting obligations. Support emails are kept for up to 12 months. Data on your phone stays until you clear the Game's data or uninstall it.

11. Children

The Last Platform is a mystery game with mild unease. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are not set to child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has used the Game or sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, port or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Because the Game has no account, most requests about your save can be handled on your device:

  • Clear the Game's data: Android Settings › Apps › The Last Platform › Storage › Clear data. This deletes the save, the backup, the settings, the ad-free entitlement and the consent choice on that device. It does not cancel a purchase; the purchase remains with your Google account and can be restored.
  • Uninstall the Game to remove the same local data.
  • Ad data: use the Google controls in section 4, or contact Google through its privacy policy. Email us and we will help you make the request.
  • Emails you sent us: ask us to delete them at support@logicandgate.com and we will do so within 30 days.

Step-by-step instructions, including what remains with Google, are on the Delete Your Data page. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to answer you first.

13. Security

The save is stored in the Game's app-private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Purchase confirmation is handled by Google Play, and ad requests use encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not operate a server that could lose your game data.

14. Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects this policy — for example a new ad format, a new purchase option or a new SDK — we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released. The Game currently includes AdMob advertising and Google Play billing.
