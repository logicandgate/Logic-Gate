Delete Your Floodbound Account

Floodbound, developed by Logic & Gate

⚠️  Deleting your account is permanent. Your islands, your raft, everything you have built and all of your progress are removed and cannot be restored.

How to request deletion

Use either of these. Both reach the same place.

Option 1 — In the game

  1. Open Floodbound and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

Option 2 — Email us

  1. Email support@logicandgate.com
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Floodbound account”.
  3. Send it from, or include, the email address you sign in with (for Google sign-in, your Google account's email), so we can find your account.

We action email requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We will email you to confirm once it is done. If we can't tell that the request comes from the account's owner, we will ask you to confirm from that email address first.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your Floodbound account and sign-in record, including your email address, your name and your Google account link
  • Your game save: your islands, raft, buildings, inventory, journal and story progress
  • Our record linking your Remove Ads purchase to your account
  • Any security-check records linked to your account

What is kept, and for how long

Your Remove Ads purchase. The purchase belongs to your Google Play account, and Google Play keeps its own record of the payment under Google's privacy policy. If you make a new Floodbound account later with the same Google Play account, you can restore Remove Ads.

Advertising data. Ads in Floodbound come from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. That data is held by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google → Ads.

Apart from these, we keep nothing about you after deletion.

More information

For full details of what we collect and why, see the Floodbound Privacy Policy.
