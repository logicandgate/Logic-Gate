2. Description of Service

Znite Run is an endless-runner game in which users complete timed runs, collect in-game coins, and claim those coins as "Z Coin" — a virtual in-app reward balance. No real-world value is created by playing; Z Coin is a virtual reward redeemable only as described in Section 6.

4. Gameplay and Earning Z Coin

4.1. Completing a run and tapping "Claim Coins" credits the coins and distance from that run to your account balance. Leaving a run without claiming forfeits that run's coins and distance.

4.2. Claiming a run's coins requires watching a rewarded video advertisement to the end. The coins are credited once our advertising partner confirms the advertisement was watched, which is usually immediate. If you close the advertisement early, or none is available, that run's coins are not credited and you may try claiming again.

4.3. Free accounts may play up to 1 hour of active in-run time within any rolling 12-hour window. A forced interstitial advertisement may appear periodically during app use for free accounts.

4.4. To protect the economy against automated abuse, the number of runs you may claim within a rolling 12-hour window is limited in proportion to your play-time allowance. Normal play does not reach this limit; if you do, claiming becomes available again as the window rolls forward.

4.5. We reserve the right to adjust earning rates, play-time limits, claim limits, or ad frequency at any time, with notice provided through the App.

5.2. Bronze removes forced interstitial advertisements during app use.

5.3. Gold includes everything in Bronze, plus: up to 3 withdrawal requests per week (instead of 1), up to 3 hours of active in-run play within any rolling 12-hour window (instead of 1 hour), and a daily Z Coin bonus of 10,000.

5.4. Subscriptions are managed through the Google Play Store and auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

5.5. Premium status expires at the end of the paid period unless renewed. You may restore previously purchased subscriptions through the App's restore-purchase feature.

5.6. See our Subscription Disclosure for a plain-language summary of what a subscription does and does not include.

5.7. Your Premium status is determined by Google Play's records, not by the App. Every purchase and every later change is verified directly with Google Play, and renewals, cancellations and expiries are applied to your account automatically, with no action required from you.

If Google Play places your subscription on account hold (for example, when a payment method fails), or you pause it, Premium access is suspended for that period and is restored automatically once the subscription is active again. During a Google Play grace period your Premium access continues uninterrupted.

6. Withdrawals

6.1. Users may request a withdrawal once their balance reaches the minimum of 2,000,000 Z Coin (equivalent to $1.00 USD at the current rate).

6.3. You are responsible for providing a correct Solana wallet address. Withdrawals sent to an incorrect address cannot be recovered.

6.4. Free and Bronze accounts may request one withdrawal every 7 days; Gold subscribers may request up to 3 per week, per Section 5.3.

6.5. The Z Coin-to-USD rate and resulting SOL amount are set by us and may be updated periodically to reflect market conditions.

6.6. We reserve the right to delay, modify, or deny any withdrawal request that violates these Terms, is suspected of fraudulent activity, or fails device-integrity verification.
