Lalitar Cha: Last Order Data and Deletion

Last updated 9 November 2026

The Game has no user accounts and no game server. There is no sign-up or login, so there is no account to delete. Your progress never leaves your phone, and Logic & Gate does not hold a profile or a save file for you. Most of what follows you can do yourself, immediately, without asking us.

This page explains how to have data relating to your use of Lalitar Cha: Last Order (package com.logicandgate.lastorder) deleted, what gets deleted, and what is kept.

How to ask us

  1. Email support@logicandgate.com from any address.
  2. Use the subject line Data deletion request: Lalitar Cha.
  3. Tell us what you want deleted. If your request concerns advertising data, include your Android Advertising ID if you are able to (Android Settings, Google, Ads).
  4. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is kept

On your phone: game progress, settings, ad choice, Remove Ads marker

Deleted the moment you uninstall the Game or clear its data (Android Settings, Apps, Lalitar Cha, Storage, Clear data). We never receive this data and cannot delete it for you.

With our advertising partners: Advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device details, ad activity, diagnostics

Held by Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads) and Google (Google AdMob). Deleted on request to the partner that holds it. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings, which breaks the link to past advertising data. You can also contact Unity or Google directly, and manage Google's ads in My Ad Center. Each partner keeps this data for the periods in its own policy. Email us and we will help you make the request.

Remove Ads purchase

Your purchase belongs to your Google account and is held by Google, so that it can be restored. Deleting the Game's data does not delete or refund it. Order records that Google shows us, such as order number, date, product, price and country, are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires, and we cannot delete them before then.

Emails you have sent us

Deleted on request. If you do not ask, they are kept only as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 12 months.

What we keep after a deletion request

Nothing beyond what the law requires, as described above. We may keep a minimal record that a deletion request was made and completed, so that we can show we honoured it. That record contains no advertising identifiers and no game data.

Your other rights

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, port or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its use, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. You can change your advertising choice at any time in the Game's Settings, under Privacy options or Personalised ads. Use the address above for any of these requests. See also the Privacy Policy.
