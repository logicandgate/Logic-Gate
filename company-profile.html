Company Overview

Logic & Gate is an independent video game studio, run as a sole proprietorship. Founded on 16 April 2026 by Solayman Islam Ruman, the studio designs, builds and publishes games for Android players worldwide.

Our current project is Dreadhush, a first-person survival horror game set in a house built over an abandoned mine. Our next game, Duskthorn, is coming soon.
