4. Mining System

4.1. Free users may initiate up to three (3) mining sessions per day. Premium users may initiate up to twelve (12) mining sessions per day.

4.2. Each mining session lasts approximately 3 hours for free users and 1.5 hours for Premium users (2x speed). Both free and Premium users earn 1,667 points per completed session.

4.3. Mining sessions start immediately and cost nothing to begin. A rewarded video advertisement (opt‑in) is shown when you claim the points of a finished session. Premium users may instead wait a 60‑second ad‑free warm‑up and claim without watching an ad. If no advertisement is available, your points are credited anyway — earned points are never withheld because an ad could not be shown.

4.4. Points earned per session are fixed at 1,667 points, which equals exactly 0.001667 TAN (1 TAN = 1,000,000 points).

4.5. The daily earning cap applies only to combined mining and ad rewards: 10,002 points for free users and 25,005 points for Premium users. Points earned from mini‑games, the Daily Mystery Box, TAN TREE, and streak bonuses are separate earning opportunities and are NOT counted against this cap.

4.6. Daily mining limits and earning caps reset at 00:00 UTC.

6. TAN TREE (Premium Feature)

6.1. TAN TREE is an exclusive feature available only to users with an active, verified Monthly or Yearly Premium subscription. Weekly subscribers and free users cannot access TAN TREE.

6.2. TAN TREE is a gamified passive‑earning feature represented as a growing crystal tree. The tree progresses through five (5) levels, with each higher level generating a greater amount of passive TAN per day.

6.3. Passive daily earnings by level:

  • Level 1: 0.001 TAN/day
  • Level 2: 0.002 TAN/day
  • Level 3: 0.004 TAN/day
  • Level 4: 0.007 TAN/day
  • Level 5: 0.012 TAN/day

6.4. Users grow their tree by performing up to three nurturing actions per day:

  • Watering: increases growth progress by 5%. May be performed up to three (3) times per day. Each watering requires watching a rewarded video advertisement.
  • Fertilizing: increases growth progress by 20%. May be performed once per day. Fertilizing costs 5,000 points (0.005 TAN), deducted directly from the user's point balance at the time of use. Fertilizing cannot be performed if the user's balance is below 5,000 points.
  • Sun Boosting: increases growth progress by 10%. May be performed once per day. Sun Boosting requires watching a rewarded video advertisement.

6.5. When a tree's growth progress reaches 100%, it automatically advances to the next level and growth progress resets to 0%. Level 5 is the maximum level; growth progress beyond 100% at Level 5 does not grant any further level increase.

6.6. TAN TREE daily action limits (Watering, Fertilizing, Sun Boosting) reset once every 24 hours. This reset is independent from, and may occur at a different time than, the 00:00 UTC reset described in Section 4.6 that applies to mining limits and earning caps.

6.7. Passive daily TAN earnings generated by TAN TREE are distributed automatically once per day through an automated scheduled process and are credited directly to the user's point balance. TAN TREE passive earnings are separate from, and not counted against, the daily mining earning caps described in Section 4.5.

The tree's level and growth progress are preserved during any such suspension and are not reset or lost.

7. Mini‑Games

The App includes three optional mini‑games accessible from the Mine screen. Each offers unique gameplay and rewards TAN points upon completion or based on performance.

7.1. Cave Mining Game – An interactive cave‑themed game where you mine TAN through 4 runs with randomized events. Points are added to your TAN balance. The game can be played once per day (reset at 00:00 UTC). All 4 runs are played without interruption; one rewarded video advertisement is shown when you claim your points at the end.

7.2. Word Catching Game – A word‑based reaction game where you catch falling letters to form words. Earn TAN based on your score. All 4 rounds are played without interruption; one rewarded video advertisement is shown when you claim your points at the end. An additional optional ad to continue after losing all lives is offered but never required. All points are added to your regular TAN balance and are separate from the mining‑and‑ad earning cap.

7.3. Gems Merging Game – A match‑3 puzzle game where you swap gems to create matches of three or more. The game consists of 4 rounds with increasing point targets (350 / 650 / 1000 / 1333 points). All 4 rounds are played without interruption; one rewarded video advertisement is shown when you claim your points at the end. Total points earned are added to your TAN balance. The game can be played once per day (reset at 00:00 UTC).

All mini‑games are optional, and point values are displayed within each game. We reserve the right to adjust mini‑game rewards or limits without prior notice.
