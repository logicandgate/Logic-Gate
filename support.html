FAQ

Dreadhush — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my progress saved? +

On your phone only. There is no account, so uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings starts you over, and progress does not move between devices.

How do I play? I keep dying. +

Read How to Play on the game's home screen. It explains the Husher, hiding, the torch and how every lock works. The answers themselves are in the notes you find in the house, and they change every day.

Why are there ads? +

Dreadhush is free. An ad plays after a death, and you can choose to watch one to refill a dead torch. That one is always optional (though true gamers never do).

Can I stop personalised ads? +

Yes. Reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads), or tap the privacy icon inside an ad. See the Dreadhush Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a bug? +

Email support@logicandgate.com with your phone model, Android version and what you were doing. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.

Response time: 1–2 business days
