Delete Your Tanzanite Mining Account

Tanzanite Mining, developed by Logic & Gate

⚠️  Deleting your account is permanent. Your TAN balance is lost and cannot be restored. If you have TAN you want to keep, withdraw it before you request deletion.

How to request deletion

Use either of these. Both reach the same place.

Option 1 — Account deletion form

  1. Open the Tanzanite Mining account deletion form.
  2. Enter the email address and miner name on your account so we can find it.
  3. Submit the form.

Option 2 — Email us

  1. Email support@logicandgate.com
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Tanzanite Mining account”.
  3. Include the email address and miner name on your account.

We action deletion requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We will email you to confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, all of the following is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials, including your email address
  • Your miner name
  • Your TAN balance and points
  • Your mining history, mining sessions and daily activity records
  • Your mini-game records — Cave Mining, Word Catch and Gems Merging
  • Your Tanzanite Tree progress and watering history
  • Your daily streak history and bonus records
  • Your referral code, referral count and referral earnings
  • Your rewarded-ad and offer reward history
  • Your purchase and subscription records
  • Your saved wallet address and auto-payout settings
  • Your withdrawal records
  • The device identifier linked to your account, which frees that device to register a new account
  • Your device verification records, including the stored IP address and device integrity results
  • Any password-reset records associated with your email address
  • Your two-factor authentication settings, if you enabled them

If you referred other players, their accounts are not affected — the link recording that you referred them is simply cleared.

What is kept, and for how long

Blockchain transactions. If you withdrew TAN as cryptocurrency, that payout was recorded on the public Solana blockchain. That record is permanent and public. It cannot be edited, hidden or deleted by us, by you, or by anyone else, because it is not stored on our servers and is not within our control. Deleting your Tanzanite Mining account has no effect on it.

Apart from the blockchain, we retain nothing about you after deletion. Our own record of your withdrawals is removed along with the rest of your account.

💡  Deleting your Tanzanite Mining account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. If you subscribed to Premium, cancel it in the Google Play Store first, or you will continue to be billed.
