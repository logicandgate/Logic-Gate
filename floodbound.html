Floodbound

The sea came up and never went back down. You wake on a broken raft beside a little island, with a crackling radio and no clear memory of how you got there. Gather, craft, patch up the raft and sail a drowned archipelago to find out who is still out there.

The Archipelago

Survive the sea. Follow the signal.

A calm survival game with no monsters and no combat — just the weather, the tide, and whatever you can build.

🪵

Gather and Craft

Driftwood, stone, palm leaves and salvage. Make an axe, a workbench, a cooking fire and a collector for rainwater.

🛶

Your Raft

Patch the hull, grow the deck plank by plank, fit a sail and an anchor, and steer it from island to island.

🏠

Build a Home

Floors, walls, doors and roofs that snap together, on land or on the raft. Take anything down and every piece comes back.

🌊

Tide and Weather

The tide uncovers sandbars full of salvage twice a day. Storms come with a warning — drop anchor before they hit.

🏝️

Six Islands

Palm groves, rocky outcrops, a drowned settlement, a lighthouse and an outer reef, each hidden on your map until you reach it.

📻

The Signal

A broken voice on an old radio. Follow it across the sea and find out what happened to the world.

Your Account

Your island goes where you go

Floodbound uses a free account — sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email — so your progress and your Remove Ads purchase are kept safe when you reinstall the game or change phones.

We collect only what the game needs to run. Read exactly what that is in the Floodbound Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and its data at any time from the game's settings or on the account deletion page.

System Requirements

Will it run on my phone?

Floodbound is still in development, so these may change before release. Recommended is the phone we test on: 60 frames a second on Medium graphics. Minimum is what the game is built to install and start on, and we haven't tried every device.

Minimum

Android Android 8.0 or newer
Processor 64-bit or 32-bit ARM
Graphics OpenGL ES 3.0 or Vulkan
Memory 3 GB RAM
Free storage 300 MB
Internet Needed to sign in

Recommended

Android Android 16
Processor MediaTek Helio G99 or better
Graphics Mali-G57 or better, Vulkan
Memory 8 GB RAM
Free storage 300 MB
Internet Stable Wi-Fi or mobile data
Questions

Is it out yet?

Not yet. Floodbound is in development and coming to Google Play for Android. This page will have the link as soon as it is out.

Is it free?

Yes, with ads. An optional one-time purchase removes them for good. Ads never block the game and are never the only way to carry on.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one, so your save and purchase are never lost. You can delete it, with everything in it, whenever you like.
