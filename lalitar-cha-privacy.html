Lalitar Cha: Last Order Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Solayman Islam Ruman, doing business as Logic & Gate (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you download and play Lalitar Cha: Last Order, our first-person horror game for Android (the “Game”). If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Game. Questions: support@logicandgate.com.

In short: Lalitar Cha: Last Order has no accounts and no sign-in, and your progress never leaves your phone. The Game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which can request ads from Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These companies collect device and advertising information to deliver and measure those ads and to prevent fraud. An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled by Google Play; we never see your card details. We do not sell your personal information for money.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Logic & Gate, a sole proprietorship owned by Solayman Islam Ruman, South Bhitabaria, Bhandaria, Pirojpur, Barisal Division 8550, Bangladesh. We are responsible for the information described in this policy that we control. Our advertising partners are responsible for the information they collect under their own privacy policies (see section 6).

2. What information is collected

Information you give us

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account details. There is no registration, no login and no chat. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

Information kept only on your device

The Game saves your progress (such as the current in-game day, your in-game money and pantry, the state of the tea stall and what has happened so far) and your settings (such as volume, graphics quality and look sensitivity) in the Game's own storage on your phone. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

If you buy Remove Ads, the Game also keeps a small marker on your phone so that the benefit works offline. The marker is tied to your phone using Android's app-specific device ID. Neither the marker nor that ID is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases through Google Play

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase, paid to and processed by Google Play under your Google account. We never receive or store your card or bank details. To unlock and restore the purchase, the Game uses the Google Play Billing Library, which asks Google Play whether your Google account owns it. The Game checks Google's answer on your phone and does not save purchase tokens or send them to us.

Like every developer on Google Play, we can see order records in our Google Play account, such as the order number, date, product, price, currency and country, and these are used for accounting, tax, refunds and support. Google's handling of your account and payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The internet check

If you play the free version, the Game checks that the internet really works before a session starts, by requesting an empty page from a Google address made for this purpose (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com or www.google.com). As with any web request, Google receives your IP address and basic connection information. The Game sends nothing else with it and does not keep the result. If you own Remove Ads, this check does not run.

Information collected by our advertising partners

The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, a mediation service of Unity Technologies. LevelPlay chooses which ad network fills each ad space; the networks used are Unity Ads and Google AdMob. When the Game requests or shows an ad, their software (built into the Game) automatically collects information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), where available, and identifiers the advertising software assigns to your device or installation;
  • your IP address, and the approximate location derived from it (such as your country);
  • device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, and the Game's name and version;
  • ad activity: which ads were requested, shown and tapped, and whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end, and the timing of your sessions in the Game;
  • diagnostic information about the advertising software, such as errors, load times and performance;
  • purchase history, which Unity lists among the data its advertising software can collect in apps with purchases.

If you own Remove Ads, the Game does not start this advertising software at all, so none of the above is collected by it.

The Game does not collect your precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone.

Analytics and crash reports

The Game contains no analytics software and no crash-reporting software of its own, and it does not send gameplay data to us. Google Play may give us combined statistics about crashes and performance, from people who have chosen to share usage and diagnostic data with Google. These statistics do not identify you to us.

3. How the information is used

  • To show ads that keep the Game free. After your character dies, the Game offers an optional rewarded ad, which you choose to watch, to carry on from where you were, and shows a full-screen ad if you choose to give up instead. Our partners use the information above to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, keep ad delivery secure and prevent ad fraud, and, where permitted, to personalise them.
  • To provide Remove Ads: to sell it through Google Play, confirm it, restore it when you reinstall, and handle refunds and our accounting and tax records.
  • To check the internet connection for free play, as described in section 2.
  • To answer you when you contact us for support.
  • To comply with the law and to protect our rights, our players and the Game.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: your consent for personalised advertising, which you can withdraw at any time; performance of our contract with you when you buy Remove Ads; our legitimate interests in funding a free game with non-personalised ads, measuring those ads and preventing fraud; and legal obligations where the law requires us to process or keep information.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Before any ad is requested, the Game shows Google's consent message, where you can consent, refuse or manage your choices. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy options. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Opting out in US states with privacy laws. You can opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information at any time in Settings › Privacy options, which opens Google's privacy form. The Game passes your choice on to Unity LevelPlay.
  • Everywhere else. The first time you open the Game, before any ad is requested, it asks whether you allow personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Personalised ads. If you say no, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Your Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads). Without it, ads cannot be personalised to you.
  • Inside an ad. Many ads show a small privacy icon you can tap to learn more or to opt out of certain personalised ads.
  • Google. You can manage the ads Google shows you in Google My Ad Center.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. The Game never requires you to watch one.
  • Remove Ads. If you buy it, no ads are shown and the advertising software is not started.

6. Who information is shared with

  • Unity Technologies (Unity LevelPlay mediation and Unity Ads), which collects and processes the advertising information described in section 2 under its own policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google AdMob), which LevelPlay can ask for ads and which collects and processes the advertising information described in section 2 under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google also provides the Android Advertising ID, distributes the Game through Google Play, and processes Remove Ads purchases.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it, and a buyer or successor if our business or the Game is ever sold or transferred.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through our partners may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some US state laws; you can opt out as described in section 5.

7. International transfers

Our advertising partners may process information in the United States and other countries, which may not have the same data protection laws as yours. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, such transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in each partner's policy.

8. How long information is kept

We do not keep any player data on our own servers. Your progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. The unlock marker stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data, and your purchase stays with your Google account so that it can be restored. Order records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 12 months. Our advertising partners keep the information they collect for the periods stated in their own policies.

9. Children

Lalitar Cha: Last Order is a horror game with frightening content. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies where you live). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada and several US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information; to object to or restrict its use; to data portability; to withdraw consent; and to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sharing” of personal information. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Because the Game has no accounts and we hold no player profile, the quickest routes are set out on our data and deletion page: clear the Game's data or uninstall it (removes everything on your device); reset or delete your Advertising ID (section 5); and, for information held by our advertising partners, the request options in the Unity privacy policy and the Google Privacy Policy. You can also email us at support@logicandgate.com and we will help. If you are in the EEA or UK and are unhappy with how we handled your request, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

We keep what we collect to a minimum, and the Game stores your progress only on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the Game or on this page.
